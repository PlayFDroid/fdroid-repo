Gravity Defied: Trial Racing is a legendary physics-based trial motorcycle racing game originally developed by Codebrew Software in 2004 for J2ME mobile devices.

This modernized Android edition faithfully preserves the authentic 2004 fixed-point physics engine, classic wireframe vector tracks, and responsive bike dynamics while introducing comprehensive updates for contemporary smartphones.

Features of this modernized edition:
• Complete collection of all 30 original classic tracks across 100cc, 175cc, 220cc, and 325cc leagues.
• Integrated mod catalog with in-game downloading and instant switching between over 1000 fan-made track packs.
• Virtual on-screen 1–9 numeric control pad with multi-touch and haptic feedback.
• Edge-to-edge layout with full support for modern display cutouts and aspect ratios.
• Complete removal of proprietary telemetry and tracking SDKs (Firebase/Crashlytics) for complete privacy and libre compliance.
• Modernized Android toolchain targeting Android 15/16+ (SDK 35/36).
• Compatible with all modern 64-bit devices and 16 KB page-size kernels.
• Free and open-source under the GPL-2.0 license.
