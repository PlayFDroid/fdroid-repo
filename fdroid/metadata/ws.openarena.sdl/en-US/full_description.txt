OpenArena is a standalone, open-source 3D first-person shooter powered by the ioquake3 / Quake III Arena engine.

Battle with classic weapons like the Rocket Launcher, Railgun, Plasma Gun, and Lightning Gun across dozens of arenas in Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Tournament, and Capture the Flag modes.

Features of this modernized Android port:
• Touchscreen controls with Ray-Traced Autoshoot (automatically fires when an enemy crosses your crosshairs).
• Authentic first-person perspective by default.
• Tap anywhere to respawn when eliminated, plus an emergency manual fire button.
• Compatible with modern Android 15/16 and 16 KB page-size Linux kernels (ARM64).
• Hardware mouse capture, physical gamepad, and configurable gyroscope aiming support.
• Integrated asset downloader fetching official game data files on first launch.
