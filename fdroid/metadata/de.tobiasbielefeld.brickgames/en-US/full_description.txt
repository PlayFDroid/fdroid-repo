Simple Brick Games recreates the nostalgic low-cost LCD brick handheld consoles (Brick Game 9999 in 1) with authentic retro charm and modern Android support.

Features:
• 14 classic mini-games including Falling Blocks, Tanks, Racing, Snake, Brick Breaker, Frogger, and Shooting.
• Authentic monochrome LCD display with customizable brick textures and backlight colors.
• Customizable handheld body shells and button color themes.
• Adjustable difficulty, starting level, and speed for each individual game.
• Separate highscore tracking for each mini-game.
• Support for physical keyboard and gamepad button mapping alongside on-screen tactile controls.
• Tactile haptic feedback modernized for contemporary Android devices.
• Modernized build targeting Android 15+ (SDK 35) with universal compatibility across all modern architectures.
• Free and open-source under the GPLv3+ license.
